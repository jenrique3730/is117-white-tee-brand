Reference → choice

V&A’s Dell Type K lamp (Christian Dell, 1929) uses a clear, functional structure. This proposal translates that clarity into a simple grid, measured rules, and open space.

Audience effect

For a person comparing everyday basics, the hierarchy may make the object, available sizes, and next step easier to scan.

Risk to watch

Too much restraint can feel clinical or generic. The accent and personal styling note add warmth without adding product claims.

Reference: V&A, “What was modernism?” · image/reference: Dell-Lampe Type K, Christian Dell, 1929. See museum context and object image.
Direction B / PostmodernistPlay · contrast · self-awareness

Reference → choice

V&A describes postmodernism as self-aware, theatrical, vivid, and complex. Bedin’s Super lamp (1981) gives a playful, composed silhouette. This preview translates that spirit with an arch, oversized numeral, and warm contrast.

Audience effect

The more expressive framing may help someone who treats a plain tee as a personal styling canvas imagine play, rather than read the page as purely utilitarian.

Risk to watch

Decoration can compete with the shirt and reduce scan clarity. The large numeral stays behind the illustration, and the offer remains unchanged.

Reference: V&A, “What is postmodernism?” · image/reference: Super lamp prototype, Martine Bedin for Memphis, 1981. See museum context and object image.
